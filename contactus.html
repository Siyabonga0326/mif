Get Involved

Your support — whether sponsorship, partnership, or volunteering — helps rural youth in KwaMaphumulo access sport, arts, culture and meaningful opportunities.

✧ How to partner with us

Mzurayza Ingwenya Foundation welcomes individuals, corporates, government departments and international partners. Here's how you can help:

  • Sponsorship — Fund a programme, event, or prize pool (Youth School Championship, Ikhono Showcase, skill workshops).
  • CSI Partnerships — Align your Corporate Social Investment with measurable rural impact.
  • Volunteer — Join our event management, mentorship or workshop facilitation teams.
  • In-kind donations — Sport kits, art supplies, sound equipment, trophies or catering.
