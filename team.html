Our leadership

Driven by community, led by youth

Every member of the MIF executive team was born in KwaMaphumulo. This isn't just a job for them — it's a mission rooted in personal experience, love for arts, sport and a deep desire to see their community thrive.

MM

Mzuvele Mkhize

Chairperson & Founder

Known in the arts and culture world as Mzurayza, Mzuvele Mkhize was born at KwaMaphumulo Ward 11. He holds a Bachelor's Degree in Management, Media and Cultural Studies from the University of KwaZulu-Natal. A passionate musician and entrepreneur, he founded Mzutainment, Ingwenya Clothing and the Mzurayza Ingwenya Foundation. Mzuvele oversees all MIF programmes and drives the organisation's vision to create lasting creative and economic impact in rural KwaZulu-Natal.

SM

Siyabonga Majozi

Secretary & IT Lead

Siyabonga grew up in Maphumulo and has been part of MIF's management team since 2022. A young IT specialist currently completing his studies at the Durban University of Technology, he manages all digital promotion, event administration and technology for MIF's programmes — including the Maphumulo Festival, Youth School Tournament and Maqadini Soccer Tournament.

PB

Philile Bhengu

Executive Management

Philile brings experience and dedication to MIF's event management. She has played a vital role in the success of multiple MIF events and continues to be a key figure in ensuring every programme runs smoothly and achieves its intended community impact.

QM

Qhawekazi Mtshali

Executive Management

Qhawekazi is one of two key young women in MIF's leadership. Her experience from previous events and her commitment to the organisation's mission make her an invaluable part of the team. She works across programme planning, logistics and community engagement.

MM

Mthetheleli Msweli

Executive Management

Mthetheleli has been part of major MIF events including the Youth School Tournament, Maphumulo Festival and Maqadini Tournament. His experience and strong working relationship with the rest of the team make him a cornerstone of MIF's operational capacity.

Our culture

How we work

Community first

Every decision we make is rooted in what's best for the youth and community of KwaMaphumulo. We are from here — we know the needs because we've lived them.

Youth-led excellence

Our team is made up of graduates and students who have earned their roles through dedication, hard work and genuine passion for arts, sport and community development.

Proactive, not reactive

We don't wait for problems to get worse. We create opportunities, platforms and safe spaces before youth become vulnerable — that's the MIF way.

Measurable impact

We track our progress — growing school enrolments, improving matric pass rates, and increasing participation are all direct outcomes of our programmes.

Cultural pride

We celebrate Zulu heritage, indigenous arts, and local identity. Our events are proud expressions of who we are — and who we can become.

Growing the team

Through our Graduate/Students Voluntary Programme, we bring in new young talent each year — mentoring the next generation of community leaders.
