Meet Our Groundbreakers

Young, passionate graduates and creators from KwaMaphumulo — people who understand this community because they grew up in it. This is homegrown leadership.

Our leadership

Rooted in Maphumulo, rising together

Every member of the MIF executive team was born in KwaMaphumulo. This isn't just a job for them — it's a mission forged in lived experience, love for arts and sport, and unwavering belief in rural youth.

Mzuvele Mkhize

Chairperson

Hailing from KwaMaphumulo Ward 11, Mzuvele holds a Bachelor's Degree in Management, Media and Cultural Studies (UKZN). As a musician, entrepreneur and cultural architect, he founded Mzutainment, Ingwenya Clothing, and Mzurayza Ingwenya Foundation (NPO). He drives the organisation's vision — turning rural creative energy into sustainable impact. His philosophy: "We don't wait for change — we create the stage."

Mthetheleli Msweli

Deputy Chairperson

A veteran of MIF's major events, Youth School Tournament, Maqadini Tournament and Maphumulo Festival, Mthetheleli turns big visions into on-the-ground realities. He holds a Higher Certificte in Health Care Management obtained from Regent Business School. His network and calm leadership make large-scale rural events possible.

Siyabonga Majozi

Secretary

Siyabonga grew up in Maphumulo and has been part of MIF's core since 2022. He is currently completing his Bachelor of Information and Communications Technology Honours at DUT. He manages digital promotion, livestreams, event systems and administration. His insight bridges rural traditions with new technology — making sure MIF stays future-ready.

Philile Bhengu

Deputy Secretary

Philile is the engine behind MIF's flawless event delivery. She is currently completing her Degree in Public Administration: Supply Chain Management at DUT. From the Youth School Championship to Maqadini Tournament, her meticulous coordination and community-first approach have elevated every programme. She embodies grace under pressure and deep empathy for rural youth.

Qhawekazi Mtshali

Treasurer

Qhawekazi brings vibrant energy and authentic connection to every MIF initiative. She is currently completing her Diploma in Business Management at Elangeni College — a qualification that directly equips her to manage the organization's finances, track budgets, and lead sponsor acquisition efforts. Her experience spans from grassroots mobilisation to high-level programme planning.

Our ethos

How we shape the future

We don't do reactive — we build proactive ecosystems of talent, pride and opportunity.

Born & Bred Community

We don't parachute solutions — we live here. Every decision is shaped by the real needs of Maphumulo.

Creative Sovereignty

We celebrate Zulu heritage, indigenous arts, and local identity — turning culture into career paths.

Proactive, Not Reactive

We create platforms before youth become vulnerable — fighting crime, GBV and substance abuse by offering purpose.

Impact Obsessed

Increased school enrolments, higher matric pass rates, and 19+ schools in our tournament — we measure what matters.

Heritage Amplified

We don't just preserve culture — we remix it for the future. Indigenous arts, Maskandi, dance and drama all have a home here.

Next-Gen Leadership

Through our Graduate/Students Voluntary Programme, we mentor young leaders from Maphumulo — building a pipeline of changemakers.
