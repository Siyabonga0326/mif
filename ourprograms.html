Sport · Education · Arts

Youth School Championship

Previously known as the Youth School Tournament, the Youth School Championship is MIF's flagship annual event. Established in 2015 by Mzuvele Mkhize together with the youth of Maphumulo, it gives primary and secondary school learners a platform to compete in soccer, netball and arts while emphasising the value of education.

The event takes place on Youth Day — 16 June — as a deliberate choice to turn the national holiday into a day of commemoration, talent and achievement rather than after-parties. It has grown from 4 secondary schools in 2015 to now including 10 secondary schools and 9 primary schools across the Maphumulo area.

In 2026, the championship expands further: group stages will run across four circuits (Balcony Hills, Mati, Mvoti and Ntonjambili) during May, with all circuit winners competing in the grand finals at Sabuyaze Sport Grounds on 16 June 2026.

Annual — 16 June (Youth Day) Sabuyaze Sport Grounds, Maphumulo 19+ schools · 300+ youth
Arts · Culture · Heritage

Maphumulo Ikhono Showcase

Ikhono means "talent" in isiZulu — and this annual event is all about celebrating it. Held on Heritage Day (24 September) at the Maphumulo Town Hall, the Showcase gives local artists and performers a competitive platform across categories including Best Young Artist, Best Female Artist, Best Male Artist, Best Maskandi, Best Gqom, Best Kwaito, Best Fine Art, Best Poetry, Best Afro Pop & RnB, and more.

The 2024 showcase — MIF's first Ikhono Showcase — was a success, with celebrity judges Mnqobi Duma (Generations: The Legacy), Londeka Mlaba (Uzalo) and DJ Luvas, alongside respected guest speakers on artist branding, entrepreneurship and heritage.

Prizes include prize money, music videos, photoshoots, trophies and certificates. The event also includes a one-hour workshop livestreamed on Facebook — making arts education accessible beyond the venue.

Annual — 24 September (Heritage Day) Maphumulo Town Hall 200+ attendees
Sport · Community

Maqadini Tournament

The Maqadini Soccer Tournament is a community football competition that creates healthy competition and social cohesion in the Maphumulo area. The tournament brings local teams together and provides a structured sporting environment for youth and community members.

Like all MIF programmes, the Maqadini Tournament is part of a broader strategy to keep young people actively engaged in sport and away from crime, substance abuse and other social ills.

Education · Skills

Skills Development & Workshops

Embedded within all MIF events are structured educational workshops covering topics like artist branding, entrepreneurship in the creative industry, digital commerce, e-commerce, SAMRO and RISA registration, and career guidance. These sessions are designed to give rural youth knowledge they cannot easily access elsewhere.

Guest speakers from the arts, business and creative industries share their journeys, challenges and practical advice — helping young people balance education and passion while building sustainable careers.

MIF also runs a Graduate/Students Project Management Voluntary Programme, giving young graduates hands-on event management experience under professional supervision.

Arts · Partnerships

Maphumulo Festival

In partnership with Mzutainment, MIF co-hosts the Maphumulo Festival — a larger multi-day arts event featuring workshops, exhibitions and live arts performances. The festival is designed to expose the Maphumulo community to cultural diversity and bring outside expertise and inspiration into the area.

The festival supports local artists by giving them a stage, connects them with industry professionals, and helps the broader community see the economic and cultural value of the arts.

Heritage · Culture

Cultural Preservation

At the heart of everything MIF does is a commitment to preserving the rich cultural heritage of KwaMaphumulo. We provide training in visual arts, drama, dance, indigenous arts and traditional music — ensuring these art forms are not lost and that young people see them as a source of pride and economic opportunity.

The theme of the 2025 Ikhono Showcase workshop — "Asivekele ubuciko Kanye nesiko lethu" (Let us preserve our arts and culture) — captures this commitment perfectly.

Our approach

Key focus areas across all programmes

1

Sport, arts and culture — The foundation of every MIF programme. We believe sport and arts are essential to educational growth. Our 2024 champions Velangezwi and Zephania are among the best matric performers in the district.

2

Health and wellness — Every major event includes health awareness content. We partner with public and private health organisations to engage youth on HIV/AIDS prevention, teenage pregnancy, and healthy living.

3

Education and opportunity — We bring learnership programmes, college opportunities and industry experts directly to our youth. Different institutes are present at events to highlight paths into creative industries and skilled employment.

4

Socio-economic development — Our events create economic activity — traders, artists, performers and vendors all benefit. We build brand awareness for local businesses and generate short-term employment for youth, women, and people with disability.

5

Fighting crime and GBV — We are a proactive organisation. By giving youth structured programmes and meaningful platforms, we reduce the time, boredom and vulnerability that lead to crime, substance abuse and gender-based violence.

6

Community cohesion — Our events are spaces for the whole community — sharing dance, networking, celebrating heritage and looking forward together. This sense of belonging and pride is transformative for rural areas.
